The Observatory Lock

“The stars remember what the living forget.”

The door before you has no handle. Instead, nine stars are carved into its surface. Four of them are dull. Five still shine faintly. Beneath them, someone has written:

“Wake the heavens in the order they vanished.”

“The Moon vanished before the Serpent.”
